# Publication canonical numbers — g1 (2026-11-07, b33 final)

## Core Concept
Point-in-time "current truth" plate for every number in the BioID NM paper at the g1 campaign (supersedes `publication-v14-numbers.md` for g1; v14 stays as the comparison arm). Each number names its source artifact under `journal/draft/data/` unless stated. Validation numbers: see "Validation" (filled after the final b33 recompute).

## Runtime and resources (final)
- **Corpus**: 15,133 MDposit deposits; 15,079 processed end to end (99.6%); 15,034 with complete stage timing (`walls_g1.jsonl`).
- **Wall time per deposit (campaign, real runs)**: median 3.8 min (226 s), p90 7.6 min, p95 10.1 min (608 s), p99 15.4 min, slowest 71 min (4,288 s); mean 4.5 min; sum 1,122 h. By input: AMBER median 4.2 min / p95 11.3 min (n = 7,825), PDB 3.4 / 7.2 min (6,822), GROMACS 4.3 / 13.8 min (387).
- **Where the time goes (share of summed wall)**: identification 73.6% (median 164 s; external + BLAST resolvers), verification 14.3% (24 s), PNG rendering 7.9% (15 s), signatures 1.9%, loading + repair + PBC + analysis + export together < 3%.
- **CPU and memory (benchmark, `bench_resources_b33.jsonl`)**: 120 deposits, 12 per campaign-runtime decile, seed 20261107, input mix 60 AMBER / 58 PDB / 2 GROMACS (corpus 52/45/3%); full production pipeline on b33 in a child process, CPU from wait4 (includes BLAST/Foldseek/PyMOL children), RAM by 1 s /proc sampling of the process tree. CPU per deposit median 281 s (4.7 CPU-min), p90 375 s, max 1,085 s; 37% system time. Peak RAM (whole tree) median 3.9 GB, p90 4.8 GB, max 6.9 GB (63k-atom system); ~3.6 GB baseline independent of system size. Mean cores in use 0.5 (p90 1.0): I/O- and service-bound, not CPU-bound. Extrapolated campaign total ≈ 1,170 CPU-hours (per-decile means × deposits). Benchmark walls are inflated by concurrent load (median 10 min) — quote campaign walls for runtime.
- **Fleet**: 24 Kubernetes workers, each limited to 4 vCPU (AMD EPYC 7543) and 20 GiB, 2 pipelines per worker (Celery threads, prefetch 1) — 48 concurrent runs; UI-submitted runs on 2 separate workers (4 vCPU / 10 GiB). Stage budgets 1,200 s for signatures, identification and verification (900 s default otherwise).
- **Reference data (local)**: PubChem mirror 124.9 M compounds (PubChem Extras 2026-11-03: SMILES, InChIKey, IUPAC, synonym flags; 35 GB SQLite) + titles file (CID-Title 2026-11-06, md5 5f9467e2…); BLAST nr 826 GiB (2026-07-26), nt 1,002 GiB, core_nt 263 GiB, Swiss-Prot 0.6 GiB, PDB-AA 0.5 GiB, PDB-NT 0.3 GiB (all snapshots 2026-07-20..28); ChEMBL 5.6 GB; Foldseek DB 2.5 GB.

## Known limits (for Discussion / reviewer answers)
- Antibodies and histones dominate UniProt misses (generic constant-region entries; near-identical paralogs).
- 226 curated small-molecule InChIKeys resolve in neither PubChem nor ChEMBL ("GT not accessible").
- Formula-family candidate ordering: ~11 curated compounds whose SMILES is a charge artifact (enolate) are found only by formula search and fall outside the 100 candidates verified (v14 found them).
- Large single-residue "ligands" (150–250 atoms, peptides labelled with one residue name) spend ~30 min in bond-order perception and keep partial signatures (45 deposits).
- Under heavy cluster load an identification stage can run out of time on protein BLAST (nr) before small molecules are searched (polymers are searched first by design); none in the campaign itself.
